# Compass — Your Situation-Aware AI Consultant

Know what changed. Understand why. Decide what's next.

Compass is an AI-powered situational intelligence platform where autonomous AI agents and humans collaboratively explore, understand, and track an evolving world.

Unlike traditional AI assistants that mainly answer questions, Compass proactively monitors developments, builds contextual understanding, and invites you to participate in the investigation.

## What it does

- Tracks real-world situations as they evolve over time.
- Connects events, evidence, and hypotheses into one living picture.
- Surfaces what changed and why it matters.
- Lets you explore, ask, and challenge the AI's understanding.

## How it works

Compass combines two ideas:

- **Living Event Graph:** a dynamic knowledge map linking events, sources, evidence, and hypotheses over time.
- **Agent Squad:** specialized AI agents that discover information, investigate events, identify connections, and test hypotheses.

Humans stay in the loop to guide investigations and sharpen results.

## Demo focus

For the hackathon, Compass demonstrates the approach using prediction-market signals and near-real-time news, with an architecture designed to support additional sources and personal applications in the future.
